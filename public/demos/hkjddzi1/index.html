How it works

Face ID is the key to a safe. Your recovery phrase is inside.

The passkey never touches a blockchain. It only locks and unlocks a backup, so the same design works on every network Kestrel supports.

  1. 1At sign-up, your phone creates your account’s recovery phrase.
  2. 2Face ID locks a backup of it. Kestrel keeps the locked copy but can’t open it.
  3. 3On a new phone, Face ID opens the backup, and everything comes back on every network.
New phoneLog in→Face ID→Your investments are back

Your passkey syncs through your Apple or Google account. If you might lose that account, add a second recovery method, an iCloud backup or a written recovery phrase, from Find out more on any holding. On Android, the phone’s biometrics (fingerprint, face unlock or screen lock) and Google Password Manager take the place of Face ID and iCloud.
